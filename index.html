Three Pillars of Principal Rekha Singh's Approach

  • Child-first teaching: Every child learns at a different pace and has unique strengths. Teachers notice individual needs early and help close learning gaps patiently rather than treating every child identically.
  • Teacher support: Teachers are regularly mentored in activity-based learning, visual teaching tools, and positive classroom management so lessons stay engaging.
  • Working closely with parents: Regular Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) keep families informed on academic progress, daily attendance, and classroom behaviour.

Classes and What Children Learn (UKG to Class 9)

Our curriculum builds foundational understanding at every stage—from joyful early learning in UKG to structured subject mastery in Class 9.

UKG (Pre-Primary)

A gentle, happy first step into school life focused on phonics, early numbers, rhymes, colouring, motor skills, and social confidence.

Primary (Classes 1 to 5)

  • English & Hindi: Reading fluency, neat handwriting, grammar, and spoken confidence
  • Mathematics: Visual aids, mental maths, and practical daily problem-solving
  • EVS: Nature, science, and social awareness through simple hands-on activities

Middle School (Classes 6 to 9)

  • General Science: Core concepts in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
  • Social Science: History, Geography, and Civics
  • Computer Literacy: Practical computer operation and digital awareness
